# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Discerning urban travelers, couples, mindful wellness seekers, small families, and intimate friend groups seeking a boutique countryside mountain retreat characterized by quiet luxury, untouched nature, and slow living.

## Product Purpose
Serve as the premier digital showcase and high-conversion direct reservation portal for VARANADAH The Farmstay. The platform conveys the sensory peace of the L-shaped estate, spacious verandah, and mountain panorama, converting visitors into direct guests.

## Positioning
A secluded boutique countryside farmhouse that fuses authentic Mangalore-tiled vernacular architecture with modern luxury comforts, centered around the wide-open verandah life, authentic regional cuisine, and uninterrupted mountain views.

## Operating Context
Visitors browsing on mobile devices and desktop screens seeking holiday inspiration, weekend getaways, and private group retreats. Immediate host access via WhatsApp and bespoke reservation requests.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Ultra-responsive Next.js 16 App Router application styled with Tailwind CSS v4.
- Configurable settings in `src/config/site.ts` (WhatsApp number, contacts, location placeholders).
- Strictly preserves factual brand rules: no invented prices, reviews, or exact addresses.
- Interactive Day / Night atmosphere toggle with ambient synthesized nature sounds (dawn birdsong and night campfire embers).
- Interactive Verandah & Architectural Layout Explorer with hotspot visual tour.
- Fullscreen Lightbox gallery, semantic metadata, OpenGraph, sitemap, and accessible booking modal.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: VARANADAH THE FARMSTAY
- Tagline: "Breath. Unwind. Rejoice."
- Cadence: PAUSE → BREATHE → CONNECT → REJOICE
- Palette: Warm ivory/cream, deep forest green, earthy dark brown, terracotta, muted olive, soft warm gold.
- Tone: Editorial, cinematic, architecture-focused, organic, and ultra-luxurious.

## Product Principles
1. Architecture as Hero: Celebrate the distinctive L-shaped design and expansive verandah as the defining signature.
2. Unhurried Calm: Generous whitespace, refined serif typography, and gentle organic transitions that make the visitor slow down.
3. Frictionless Hospitality: Direct host communication via WhatsApp and personal attention for every inquiry.
