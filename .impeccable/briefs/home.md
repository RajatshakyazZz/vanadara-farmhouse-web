# Surface Brief: Home

## Scope
Homepage of VARANADAH The Farmstay (`src/app/page.tsx`).

## Visitor Mode
Persuade & Experience. The visitor evaluates a luxury retreat stay and is guided into direct booking inquiry or WhatsApp host connection.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
An ultra-luxury architectural sanctuary centered on the verandah life. It refuses generic resort grid templates and superficial booking carousels in favor of an immersive, slow-living editorial experience that honors the property's unique L-shaped design and mountain atmosphere.

### OWN-WORLD
Deep forest greens (#0E1A14), ancient timber browns (#221813), warm ivory ground (#FAF7F2), terracotta highlights (#BA563B), and antique gold (#C8A856). Architecture-first composition, high-contrast serif typography, and tactile hairline borders.

### STORY
The visitor experiences: PAUSE → BREATHE → CONNECT → REJOICE. They explore the spacious verandah, toggle between dawn mist and starlit campfire evenings with ambient soundscapes, inspect the suites, and initiate direct host reservations.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Fullscreen cinematic 100vh hero with the actual L-shaped farmhouse estate against misty morning mountains, subtle dark earthy gradient, elegant serif brand typography, dual CTAs ("BOOK YOUR STAY" and "EXPLORE VARANADAH"), and live ambient soundscape toggle.

### FORM
Architectural sanctuary with interactive Day/Night atmosphere switcher and Verandah Hotspot Explorer. Seed: `varanadah-architectural-sanctuary`.

### FINISH
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
