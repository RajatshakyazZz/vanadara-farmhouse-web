# Design System

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Direction
Ultra-Luxury Architectural Sanctuary — A calm, cinematic, and architecture-focused digital presence designed to reflect the slow-living philosophy of VARANADAH The Farmstay.

## Color Palette & Tokens
- **Canvas / Ground**: `#FAF7F2` (Warm Ivory / Sand-50)
- **Secondary Ground**: `#F4EFE6` (Warm Parchment / Sand-100)
- **Deep Sanctuary Green**: `#0E1A14`, `#16261E`, `#253C2E` (Forest-950, 900, 700)
- **Ancient Timber Brown**: `#221813`, `#35251C`, `#4D3C32` (Earth-950, 900, 700)
- **Terracotta Accent**: `#BA563B` (Terracotta-500), `#A1452D` (Terracotta-600)
- **Muted Olive**: `#5A664E` (Olive-600), `#717E61` (Olive-500)
- **Antique Gold**: `#C8A856` (Gold-500), `#D8BA74` (Gold-400)

## Typography
- **Headings & Display**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Google Fonts), serif. Generous tracking on uppercase titles (`tracking-[0.22em]` to `tracking-[0.3em]`), delicate italics for emphasis.
- **Body & Captions**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Google Fonts), sans-serif. Clean, humanist, warm letterforms with 1.6 to 1.75 line-height for comfortable editorial reading.
- **Micro-labels & Coordinates**: Uppercase monospace or tracking-widened sans (`tracking-[0.25em] text-[10px] sm:text-[11px] font-medium`).

## Layout & Spatial Rhythm
- **Editorial Grids**: Generous whitespace with 96px–128px vertical section padding (`py-24 sm:py-32`).
- **Asymmetric Composition**: 12-column variable spans for experience cards, breaking repetitive card templates.
- **Full-Bleed Viewports**: Fullscreen 100vh hero and expansive 16:9 architectural viewports.

## Signatures & Micro-Interactions
- **Web Audio Ambiance**: Native Web Audio API synthesizer offering real-time mountain breeze/birdsong for dawn and campfire crackle for dusk.
- **Interactive Verandah Hotspots**: Pulsing spatial pins situated over the actual verandah photograph revealing intimate alcove details and sensory notes.
- **Interactive Suite Explorer**: Live tabbed room specification and architectural features showcase.
- **Accessible Lightbox**: Keyboard-navigated fullscreen gallery viewer with category filters.
- **Motion**: Decelerated exponential ease-out transitions (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), zero bounce easing.
